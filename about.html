Who we are

A multidisciplinary advisory partner

Zelela Trading PLC is a professional consulting and advisory firm focused on strengthening institutions, supporting enterprise growth, generating actionable research, and improving financial and organizational performance.

We work with businesses, government bodies, NGOs, social organizations, MSMEs, entrepreneurs, development partners and communities — bringing together research, health, finance, entrepreneurship, strategy and sustainability expertise under a single, integrated consulting platform.

Headquartered in Dessie, Ethiopia, Zelela is built around a simple premise: durable institutional change comes from evidence, discipline and locally grounded expertise — not templates borrowed from elsewhere.

Our Vision

To be a premier East African consulting and advisory firm, driving sustainable institutional growth, economic formalization, and social impact through integrated professional services.

Our Mission

Zelela Trading PLC delivers professional consultancy, enterprise support, financial management, and research services to businesses, government bodies, and social organizations — empowering institutions to optimize operations, navigate regulatory and financial frameworks, and execute data-driven strategies that expand opportunity across communities.

What we stand for

Our values

  • Evidence over assumption

  • Local knowledge, regional ambition
  • Long-term institutional value over short-term output
Who we serve

Clients across sectors and scale

Our engagements span a wide range of institutions, each with distinct governance, funding and accountability requirements:

  • Private businesses and corporations seeking strategic or financial advisory support
  • Government institutions strengthening planning, policy and service delivery
  • NGOs and social organizations requiring research, evaluation or compliance expertise
  • MSMEs and entrepreneurs building toward formalization and sustainable growth
  • Development partners and communities pursuing measurable social impact
